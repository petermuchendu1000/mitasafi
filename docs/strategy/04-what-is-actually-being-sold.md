# What Is Actually Being Sold

*After nine research reports. This is the product definition they converge on.*

Date: 2026-10-19

---

## The observation

Look at what every single player in this market charges for. Not what they say they do — what
you have to pay before you get anything.

| Who | What they charge | What you get for it |
|---|---|---|
| @makejani_home (TikTok) | **KSh 1,000** | "the exact property pin and direct contact with the onsite caretaker" |
| @dreamrentals1 (TikTok) | **5% of the rent** | "the exact location and the Management (Caretaker) number" — and explicitly *"I will NOT be taking you to view the house personally"* |
| StayNai | **KSh 299/month** | unlocks "full addresses, GPS pins, direct caretaker phone numbers" |
| 254rent | **KSh 1,500** | a viewing |
| @ScoutNRB | **KSh 2,000** | a viewing |
| Hao Finder | **KSh 5,000** flat under 30k rent | a search; states outright its services are "NOT Free" |
| 12 of 24 verified TikTok agent bios | **KSh 2,000 is the modal fee** | varies |

Read the second row again. He is not selling a viewing. He says in writing that he will not take you
to the house. **He is selling a phone number and a map pin.**

> **The product in this market is the location pin and the caretaker's phone number.**
>
> Everything else — the photos, the "viewing", the "verification" — is wrapping.

And now look at what people ask for, unprompted, in the comments under those same listings:
**"location?"** — 32 times in 1,425 harvested comments, and **16 of those times it is the entire
comment.** Nothing else. Just the word.

People are begging, in public, for the exact field that is deliberately withheld in order to price
it.

---

## So the product is three words long

> **The pin and the number. Free. With a date on it.**

Each of the three parts is now backed by evidence rather than reasoning.

**1. The pin and the caretaker's number.**
These are the two withheld fields. We know what they are worth because the market publishes the
price: KSh 1,000, or 5% of rent, or KSh 299 a month. Give away exactly those two things and there is
nothing left to charge for.

**2. Free.**
Not as a moral position — as the only position competitors cannot copy. Every live Kenyan product
monetises by gating these two fields. StayNai correctly worked out that the caretaker's number is
the product, then put it behind a subscription. Kejanova escrows the viewing fee. 254rent charges
it. Hao Finder charges KSh 5,000 and says so plainly. **A competitor who copies "free" destroys
their own revenue line.** That is the whole moat, and it costs nothing to hold.

**3. With a date on it.**
Someone asked *"still available?"* on a **January 2024** listing in **October 2026** — thirty-three
months later. **153 of 1,425 comments** contained an availability word. This is not a small
annoyance; it is most of the pain.

> **The product is not a list of houses. It is a date.**

---

## This fixes the verification problem by shrinking it

Every version of this plan drowned in verification. Verify the owner? No public register exists.
Verify the price? The resident who tells you gets punished. Verify the listing? It expires in days.

But if the product is just the pin and the caretaker's number, there is exactly **one** fact to
verify:

> **Does this phone number belong to the caretaker of this building?**

One fact. Not ownership, not legal authority, not price. And it is a fact that:

- **Residents can confirm safely.** No price, no name, no exposure. "Yes, that's our caretaker" costs
  a tenant nothing, embarrasses nobody, and reveals no rent. It is the one thing the research said
  residents can give without risk.
- **Is stable.** A caretaker lasts years. Verify once per building, not once per listing.
- **Is self-correcting.** A wrong number gets reported by the first seeker who calls it.
- **Cannot be faked by the A4-sheet trick.** You can tape a fake sign to a gate. You cannot make
  four residents of that building agree a stranger is their caretaker.

**So the GG House idea was right. It was just pointed at the wrong field.** Not the price — the
caretaker's number.

---

## A correction to the earlier design

Earlier documents said: *publish the number written on the sign, visible in the photo.*

**That was wrong, and the evidence shows why. The number on the sign is usually the agent's.** The
number being sold for KSh 1,000 is the **caretaker's**. They are different numbers, and the entire
product hinges on which one you publish.

Publish the sign's number and you are a free lead funnel into the agent's viewing fee — which is
exactly the attack the red team ranked as the most dangerous, and the one the founder would misread
as traction.

Publish the caretaker's number and the fee has nowhere left to attach.

## The artifact to lead with

One real TikTok caption, from @kayhomes, for a bedsitter in Ruai:

> *Rent 6k · Deposit 6k · Water 200 per unit · Garbage collection 200 per month · NB: VIEWING FEE
> APPLICABLE (2k)*

**KSh 14,000 to move into a KSh 6,000 house.**

That single line is the whole case. It needs no argument, no statistics and no pitch — it is an
agent's own advert. Put it at the top of everything.

## How to write it, measured rather than guessed

From 1,425 real comments, with counts. This overrides every earlier guess in these documents about
language, including mine.

- **"nataka" (34) beats "natafuta" (3).** Write *"I want"*, not *"I'm looking for"*.
- **"bedsitter" (42) beats "keja" (2).** **Do not over-Sheng it.** People type the plain word. Sheng
  is for speech, not for the interface.
- **"ama" ("or") is everywhere.** People think in groups of interchangeable estates — *"Kasarani ama
  Roysambu ama Githurai"*. So search must allow several estates at once. A single-estate filter is
  the wrong shape.
- **Locations are landmarks and matatu stages, never addresses.** *"nyuma ya Naivas."* Build for that
  and forget street names.
- **#housetourkenya is the wrong hashtag** — it has been taken over by people selling KSh 20m–68m
  mansions. Use **#bedsitter**.

## Two things the crowd already does for free

**It fact-checks.** The single most-liked comment found, at **502 likes**, corrects estate-name
inflation: *"Githurai 44 nyuma ya naivas ndio mnaita Roysambu"* — *you're calling Githurai 44 behind
Naivas "Roysambu"*. Nobody asked them to. Nobody paid them. That is community verification already
running at high engagement, and it is the clearest proof the core mechanism works.

**It demands this product out loud.** When a creator drifts upmarket the audience revolts:
*"Rudi kwa singles na bedsitters na 1 bedrooms"* (121 likes) — *come back to singles and bedsitters* —
and *"you've started losing the plot"* (102 likes). The mass market is actively asking to be served
and is being abandoned for mansion content.

## What is still missing

Stated honestly, because these gaps decide whether this works:

1. **Zero verified prices for Pipeline, Kayole, Umoja, Donholm, Syokimau, Kasarani, or any town
   outside Nairobi.** The TikTok sample landed on the Thika Road corridor because a proxy blocked
   the crawl. This is a sampling accident, not evidence of absence — but nothing here should be used
   to price those estates.
2. **No first-person account of how long a search takes or what it costs in total.** The time horizon
   in comments is *kesho* — tomorrow. Everything about duration remains inference and needs
   interviews.
3. **The two TikTok accounts above were read by a research agent and not independently
   re-verified.** Check @makejani_home and @dreamrentals1 yourself. It takes under a minute, and the
   entire product definition in this document rests on them.
4. **Still unknown: does a caretaker want his number published?** The whole design now turns on
   this. There is no evidence either way in nine reports. **This is the one question to ask, and it
   takes one conversation.**

---

## The question that now decides everything

Not "will people contribute?" Not "can we verify?" Not "how do we grow?"

> **"Would you want your number on a free list that sends you tenants, with no agent in between?"**

Ask five caretakers. If yes, the product is the pin and the number, free, with a date — and
everything in these documents falls into place behind it.

If no, the whole plan needs rebuilding from a different source, and better to learn that over one
afternoon than over one year.
